The Opportunity

Nigeria is a sports superpower
without a digital spine.

Nigeria has produced Olympic champions, World Cup participants, and grassroots talent across 200 million people — yet the infrastructure to identify, develop, track, and fund that talent is entirely fragmented.

"A country of 210 million with 1.4 million registered athletes and no single place to find, fund, or manage any of them."

— Platform Assessment, NSC

This gap costs the nation in missed Olympic medals, under-funded academies, idle stadiums, and billions in wasted sports grants that can't be tracked.

Problem Statement

Four systemic failures
holding us back.

🕵️

Invisible Talent

Thousands of elite-potential athletes in Kano, Aba, and Calabar go undiscovered each year because there is no searchable, verified national talent registry accessible to scouts and federations.

💸

Untracked Funding

₦14.2 billion in sports grants and sponsorships flow without a transparent digital ledger, causing misallocation, duplication, and eroded stakeholder trust.

🏟️

Idle Infrastructure

8,940 facilities across 36 states have no real-time availability, booking, or condition-tracking system — assets worth billions sit underutilised and unmaintained.

📋

Manual Governance

NSC compliance, federation reporting, anti-doping audits, and policy dissemination run on spreadsheets, email, and phone calls — adding months of latency to decisions.

⚡ Performance

Edge-deployed globally. Sub-200ms page loads for users in Abuja, Lagos, Port Harcourt, and Kano simultaneously — even during major events.

💰 Cost Efficiency

Serverless Postgres + Vercel means zero idle server cost. Infrastructure scales automatically — saving an estimated 65% vs. traditional cloud hosting.

🔒 Security & Auth

NIN-linked identity verification, JWT-based session management, role-based access control for NSC, state, federation, scout, and public roles.

🔮 Future-Proof

Architecture is API-first, making it trivial to add mobile apps, third-party integrations, and AI-powered analytics layers as the platform matures.
